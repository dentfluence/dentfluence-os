My Day: owner and manager see the clinic's tasks, not just their own

Reverses a call made earlier the same day, because it was wrong.

MyDayController forced assigned_to = me for everyone, on the reasoning
that My Day is one person's shift. For an owner or a manager that
reasoning is backwards: their shift IS the clinic, and a page that hid
the team's work from the person accountable for it just sent them to
/tasks every morning to find out what was actually happening.

Found on production: /tasks showed "Today 5" (all belonging to Ashwini
and Samiksha) while My Day showed 2. Both numbers were correct and the
pair read as broken, which is the real cost.

WHOSE TASKS is now User::taskScope() — the same boundary /tasks and the
phone use. Owner and manager see the branch; everyone else sees their
own. One answer to "whose work may I see", so My Day cannot become a
way around the permission fix that shipped an hour ago.

The section hint is no longer fixed copy: "Across the clinic" for
owner and manager, "Assigned to you" for everyone else. "Assigned to
you" printed above the whole clinic's work is how a number stops being
trusted — it is what prompted this.

MyDayQueue::tasks() (the summary source, currently unreachable behind
the board) kept in step, so swapping the board back out cannot quietly
restore the old behaviour.

CALLS NEEDED NO CHANGE. TodayActionsEngine and TodayCallList carry no
per-user filter at all — a call belongs to whoever picks up the phone,
which is what the board's own Handled-by column says: "Nobody is
assigned in advance — the desk on shift owns the board." Everyone
already sees every call, and scoping calls per person would be a
different and much larger change to the PRE engine.

Tests: MyDayTaskBoardTest — the old "a colleague's task stays off My
Day even for an admin" is now "an owner sees the whole clinic on My
Day", plus a new test that an assistant still sees only her own and
reads the right hint.
