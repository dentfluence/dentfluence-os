My Day: take Stock off the page, and stop querying what no section shows

CEO ruling, 23 Sep: "remove low Stock or inventory update from task".

WHY IT GOES
Stock ran last on My Day, below the CEO's four sections. It was the one
section on the page nobody was going to act on the same day. Reordering is a
buying decision — a quantity, a supplier, a price, usually a call to the rep —
and none of that happens between two patients. A work list that carries a row
you cannot finish today teaches people to skim the list, which costs more than
the row is worth.

Low stock has not gone anywhere. It is still on the Daily Huddle and in
Inventory, which is where the reorder decision is actually made.

WHAT CHANGED
config/my_day.php
  - The 'stock' band is removed. My Day is now exactly the CEO's four:
    calls → tasks → lab → payments.
  - The removal is documented in the header with how to undo it.

app/Services/MyDay/MyDayQueue.php
  - collect() now builds sources ON DEMAND: only the sources a configured
    band actually names are queried. Before, every source ran on every page
    load regardless of what was shown, so a section switched off in config
    still cost its query.
  - lowStock() is KEPT WHOLE, not deleted, and marked parked. Turning the
    section back on is adding the band back to config — nothing else.

resources/views/my-day/index.blade.php, _rows.blade.php
  - Empty-state and comment copy no longer promise a stock section.
  - The 'stock' row label stays in the match, so re-enabling needs no view
    change.

tests/Feature/MyDay/MyDaySectionOrderTest.php
  - The order assertion is now the four sections.
  - test_stock_is_not_on_my_day() — no band may name 'low_stock'. This is the
    guard against it drifting back in.
  - test_a_source_no_section_asks_for_is_never_queried() — the section being
    off means its query is off.

NOT CHANGED
  - Inventory, the Huddle's stock alerts, and MyDayQueue::lowStock() itself.
  - The "← Back to My Day" bar on the product page: harmless while nothing
    links there, and needed the moment the band comes back.

RUN
  php artisan test --filter=MyDay
