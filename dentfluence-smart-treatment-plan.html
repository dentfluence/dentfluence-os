02 · Your Treatment Plan

Your Treatment Plan

Your treatment has been planned in stages to restore the health, function and appearance of your teeth.

Because several back teeth are missing, your front teeth have been taking excessive load. This has contributed to severe wear and fracture.

Simply replacing the front teeth permanently would not address the underlying problem.

Your treatment therefore starts by creating a stable foundation and then rebuilding the missing support before completing the final teeth.
