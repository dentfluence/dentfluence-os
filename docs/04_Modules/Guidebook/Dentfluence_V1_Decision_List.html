C1a Meta's clock is not your clock — and it hasn't started Start today
You said"Need this channel full proof before launch, but expecting to rise by 14th September. This is going to be a differentiating feature."

Getting a WhatsApp Business number live is a paperwork chain with external waiting built into every link, and today the clinic holds none of it — no phone number ID, no business account ID, no token. Each step below can bounce back for a document mismatch or a template wording rejection, and each bounce restarts that step's clock.

StepWho controls the clockTypical wait
Meta Business verification (documents, business name match)MetaDays to a few weeks — the long pole, and the one that bounces

7 message templates submitted and approvedMeta24–48h each, rejections common on wording
Credentials into production configYouUnder an hour
Controlled first sendYouOne supervised day

There are 17 calendar days to 14 September. If verification runs long — and it is the step most likely to — no amount of development recovers it.

The one thing to do today

Start Meta Business verification now, before any of the code decisions in this document. It is not development work — it is documents, and it is the only item on the whole V1 plan where waiting is someone else's decision. Everything else in this log can be done in the time the verification is sitting in a queue.

C1b "Full proof" can be achieved before Meta answers — that's the good news Do this in parallel

Practice mode is not a limitation here, it is the test rig. Every message is composed in full, run through the consent gate, and written to the patient's record — it is only the final handover to Meta that is skipped. So you can prove correctness completely without credentials.

The proving tools already exist as commands: whatsapp:test for a plain message and whatsapp:template for a template, both described in the code as dry-run safe.

What "full proof" should mean — proposed, so it's a bar you can actually clear

  • Right person. Every send resolves to the intended patient and the correctly formatted number, including numbers stored without a country code.
  • Right words. All seven templates render with real patient data — name, date, time, amount — with no blank or wrong variable.
  • Right permission. A patient who has not granted WhatsApp consent is refused. A patient who granted service consent but not marketing is refused the promotional template and allowed the reminder.
  • No duplicates. Running the same job twice does not queue the same message twice.
  • Right record. Every attempt appears on the patient's timeline whether it sent, was refused by consent, or failed.
  • Fails safely. With bad credentials the app logs and moves on — it does not crash a scheduled job or block reception.

Six checks, no Meta account required. Do them next week and the day the credentials arrive is a configuration change, not a gamble.

Why this matters to how you sell it

Recall is the one that brings patients back — and it is manual. On 14 September, switching WhatsApp on gives you automatic appointment reminders and automatic review requests. Everything else is a button a human presses.

That is still a real differentiator against a clinic doing none of it, and manual-with-one-click is a perfectly good V1. But if the pitch is "the system chases your patients for you", the honest version today is "the system tells your reception exactly who to chase, and gives them one button to do it".

Automating recall sends is a V1.1 conversation, not a V1 one — and it should not be started inside this freeze.
