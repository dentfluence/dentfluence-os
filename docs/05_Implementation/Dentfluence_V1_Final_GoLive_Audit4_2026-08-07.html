GO*Final Verdict (with conditions)
The honest assessment against the real benchmark. The ecosystem Tulip would be replacing — Sheets + WhatsApp + paper + PMS — has three virtues no software audit should dismiss: it never crashes, it needs no training, and it works when the internet doesn't. Its fatal weaknesses are the ones Dr. Aggarwal's own webinar admits: everything depends on disciplined humans, nothing is enforced, nothing is audited, follow-ups die with staff turnover, and the owner sees only what someone remembered to type into a cell. Dentfluence V1 wins where the manual ecosystem structurally cannot: one-patient-one-record enforced by the database instead of discipline, follow-ups that create themselves as assigned tasks, money that cannot be recorded outside a locked transactional trail, a tamper-checked audit chain, and duplicate/overlap/race protection that a spreadsheet will never have. The manual ecosystem currently wins on exactly three things: it has backups-by-redundancy (paper doesn't disappear when a disk dies — Dentfluence has NO backups today), it degrades gracefully (Dentfluence has zero offline capability), and it is already trained-in. Two of those three are closable in days; the third is a training week. That is the whole gap.

Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS. 4 P0 + 7 P1, all environment/plumbing-level, closable in ~7 working days; supervised cutover Day 11; nothing requires redesign. Detailed support in every tab.
